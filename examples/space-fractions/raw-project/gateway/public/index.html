Space Fractions · A fraction adventure for sixth graders

Sign in

Paste an OIDC access token (Bearer) to authenticate. In the Compose setup, sign in through Keycloak and paste the access token here.

How to play

  • From the main menu, choose Play to start a new game.
  • Each question shows a fraction prompt and several options.
  • Write the answer as a fraction, e.g. 1/2 or 3/4.
  • You can pause a game any time and resume later.
  • Each correct answer earns 1 point. Answering again won't change your score.
  • View your score from the main menu.
  • When you answer every question, you'll see your ending feedback.
