Space Fractions

Blast through the galaxy by adding and subtracting fractions. Answer with a single reduced fraction like 3/4 or -1/2. Solve the whole mission, then check your final score.

How to play

  • Write answers as a single reduced fraction (e.g. 3/4).
  • A whole number is written as 5/1.
  • Pause and resume anytime; you cannot answer while paused.
  • Each correct answer earns 1 point. Repeats do not add points.
